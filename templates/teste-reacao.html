How it works

  1. Press Start.
  2. Wait for the box to change to Now! (its colors invert and its border thickens).
  3. Click, tap the box or press the space bar as quickly as possible.

Responding before the signal or in less than 100 ms counts as anticipation and the attempt is repeated. The measured time includes display and device delays.

About this test

Inspiration
Inspired by the psychomotor vigilance test (PVT), part of the Cognition battery used in astronaut research. Research PVTs are longer and controlled.
Limitations
This is not the validated PVT or a medical examination. A single result does not indicate fatigue, disease or fitness for work.
Accessibility
This test requires seeing the signal on the screen. Anyone unable to take it can skip it; the dashboard still works without it.

Simulated data for demonstration. This system does not provide a diagnosis.
