          About the data

          Where the data comes from
          The astronaut and all records are fictional and created for this demonstration. No sensor, watch or EEG device is connected, and there is no real 24-hour monitoring.
          How to read the colors
          Each status has its own text and icon. Green only means that no illustrative rule was triggered by the available data; it does not prove health. Insufficient data is never shown as green.
          Limitations
          Assessments and guidance are examples for this prototype. They are not diagnoses, are not clinically validated, and do not send actual alerts to the medical team.
          References
          The three-night sleep scenario follows the Project Raga planning document. For context on sleep and fatigue during missions, the team consulted NASA's fundamentals of human health and fatigue countermeasures laboratory. These sources do not validate Raga's rules.
