About check-ins

Purpose
Record how you are doing to notice changes over time. Inspired by sleep and mood questionnaires used in NASA research.
Limitations
These are not psychological tests or diagnoses and do not replace a conversation with the mission psychologist or medical team.
If you had a very difficult day
Do not wait for the next check-in: contact the mission medical team or psychologist. This prototype does not send alerts.
